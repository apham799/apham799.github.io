About

I’m a pre-doctoral M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Chicago, working with Professor Ken Nakagaki in the Actuated Experience Lab. I build interactive interfaces that connect technological advancements with physically manipulable interfaces, enabling users to take advantage of computational capabilities while retaining their sense of self.

So far my work has explored actuated and shape-changing interfaces, from room-scale balloons to millimeter-scale magnetic particles. Going forward, I’m excited about integrating AI into the loop to allow people to benefit from the physical augmentation and assistance of physically actuated interfaces while retaining their sense of self-agency.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science (HCI specialization) and B.A. in Economics from UChicago in 2025.
